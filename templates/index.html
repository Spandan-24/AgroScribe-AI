Frequently Asked
How accurate is the disease detection?
Our AI model is trained on 38+ disease classes with ~96% validation accuracy. Always confirm severe cases with a local agri-expert before heavy chemical use.
Is my uploaded image stored?
Images are temporarily stored only to generate your diagnosis and improve future model accuracy. You can request deletion anytime via email.
Where can I buy the recommended pesticides?
Check the "Nearby Pesticide Shops" section — select your district to see verified local agro-stores stocking these products.
App not detecting correctly?
Use a clear, well-lit close-up photo of the affected leaf against a plain background. Avoid blurry or shadowed images for best results.
